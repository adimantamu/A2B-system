Not a portal. Not a chatbot. How people work in the age of AI.

One link. Every AI. Your customers use Claude, ChatGPT, Copilot or Grok — one link on your page opens the one they already have, carrying your intake. Nothing for them to download, and no change to the rest of your site.

Your sections travel in the link itself — your questions, in your wording, in your order — so their AI has nothing to look up and nothing to guess at. Edit a section here and the next customer who clicks gets the edit.
